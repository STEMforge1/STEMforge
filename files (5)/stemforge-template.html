About Our Teaching

STEMforge focuses on MCAT preparation and supplemental STEM tutoring for various subjects. Our tutors bring deep expertise and years of experience teaching college and adult learners.

Most tutoring reinforces memorization and teaching content. We identify what's actually broken in one's understanding/reasoning and fix it. Misconceptions don't just disappear, they have to be actively restructured. That's where real learning happens.

Real understanding takes time. We're structured to make that time count. We help each learner focus on the concepts that matter most, and we make sure each one lands clearly before moving on.
